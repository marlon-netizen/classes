Divine Revelation and the Magisterium

How God Hands On His Truth in an Ordered Way

The Founder's Letter

Imagine a founder leaves behind a letter. Years later, people disagree about what it means. Who decides what the message means?

Keep this question in mind. The Church faces a similar question about God's Word, and the answer shows how God is a God of order, not confusion.

The Virtue of Order

God does not leave us to guess. He hands on His truth in an ordered way: He reveals Himself, the Apostles pass it on, it is written down, and the Church guards and explains it. "God is not a God of disorder but of peace" (1 Corinthians 14:33).

The Analogy of Authority

A national constitution can help us understand how God's Word is handed on.

FeatureNational ConstitutionDivine Revelation
Written textThe ConstitutionSacred Scripture
ContextFounding Fathers' intentSacred Tradition
InterpreterSupreme CourtThe Magisterium
Limits of the analogy: a constitution is a human document and a court is a human institution. Christians also trust the guidance of the Holy Spirit, who is with the Church. The Magisterium does not stand above God's Word; it serves it.
