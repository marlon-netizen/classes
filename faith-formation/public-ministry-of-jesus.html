The Public Ministry of Jesus

Identity, Mission, and the Virtue of Order

Who do people say I am?

How do your friends, classmates, or social media followers describe you? Jesus once asked His disciples, "Who do people say that I am?" (Mark 8:27). His public life shows us who He really is, and it helps us see who we really are.

Standard A.6.7.3: Mysteries of Christ's public life: baptism, temptations, announcement of the kingdom, transfiguration, ascent to and entry into Jerusalem.
By the end of this page you can: list the five mysteries of Christ's public life in order, and explain how His Baptism reveals His identity.

The Virtue of Order

Order means putting first things first in our lives. Jesus' public life unfolds in an ordered path: first He receives His identity at the Jordan, then He is tested in the desert about His priorities, then He announces the Kingdom, is revealed in glory, and goes up to Jerusalem.

The Five Mysteries at a glance

MysteryWhat it reveals
1. BaptismIdentity as the Beloved Son

Memory hook: Beloved · Tested · Kingdom · Transfigured · Jerusalem.

Living an ordered life

Prayer first
Starting the day with God, not a screen.
Integrity
Choosing what is right over what is popular.
Service
Putting the needs of others before comfort.

Real-life connection: social media shows who others say you are. At the Jordan, the Father says "beloved Son." Temptations are about ordering priorities.
