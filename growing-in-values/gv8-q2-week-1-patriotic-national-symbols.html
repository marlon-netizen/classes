The Symbols That Represent Nicaragua

Guiding question

“Why do the symbols of my country deserve my respect?”

Learning target: I can name Nicaragua's patriotic and national symbols, explain what each one means and a key fact about its history, and say why each one deserves respect.

Unit indicator: Shows respect for the patriotic and national symbols in the family, the school and the community.  ·  Textbook: Creciendo en Valores 8, pp. 88–96

What is a national symbol?

Every nation chooses symbols to express what matters most about the country: a piece of its landscape, its animals and plants, or its values. Almost every country has an anthem, a flag and a coat of arms. Some also choose a flower, a tree or a bird.

Nicaraguan citizens love their country and its symbols, because they represent us inside and outside the country. That is why it is our responsibility to feel and to show respect for them.

Two families of symbols

Patriotic symbols (Símbolos Patrios)The Flag, the Coat of Arms and the National Anthem.
National symbols (Símbolos Nacionales)The Sacuanjoche (flower), the Madroño (tree) and the Guardabarranco (bird).
