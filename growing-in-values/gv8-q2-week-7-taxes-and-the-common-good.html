Taxes and the Common Good

Guiding question

“How do taxes help my family, my school and my community?”

Learning target: I can explain what taxes, tributes and the taxing power are, describe the stages of the budget, share a simulated budget with reasons, and argue how taxes contribute to the common good.

Unit indicator: Values the importance of taxes in the common well-being of the family, the school and the community.  ·  Textbook: Creciendo en Valores 8, pp. 109–116

Taxes build the common good

Fiscal education teaches why taxes exist and what they are for, so that we understand our duty to pay them and to take part as active, responsible citizens. Taxation (tributación) is when citizens hand over to the State a certain amount of money so that the State can provide services such as education, health, roads and old-age pensions.

“The money obtained from the payment of taxes is used for improvements of the country and the well-being of the population.” (textbook, p. 110)

Key terms

Tribute (tributo)Payments of money that the State demands, in exercise of its power, to obtain resources for its aims. It brings together taxes (impuestos), fees (tasas) and contributions, set by law.
Taxing power (potestad tributaria)The faculty of the State to create, modify or suppress tributes. It is exercised by the Legislative Power and set by law.
Budget (presupuesto)A yearly plan of expected income and planned expenses and investments. It is the basis for transparency in the use of public resources.
