I take care of my life and the lives of others

Safe traffic depends on what each of us does. Every day we travel in three roles: as a pedestrian (on foot), as a passenger and, one day, as a driver. Each role has rules that protect lives.

“Be careful, preserve your life and that of others. Before crossing the street, make sure to look both ways.” (textbook, p. 117)

Three roles, three sets of responsibilities

PedestrianEveryone who travels on foot is a pedestrian, including people who push a bicycle or a motorcycle with the engine off and people who move in a wheelchair. Pedestrians must take precautions and respect the rules for crossing.
PassengerWait for transport in safe places (the sidewalk, not the roadway), do not get on or off a moving bus, wear the seat belt, and do not distract the driver.
DriverDrives at moderate speed, takes no risks, never drives after drinking, respects signals and the rights of others, and avoids distractions such as the phone.

The traffic light: red = stop, yellow = caution, green = go (when it is clear).
