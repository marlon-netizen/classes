Responsible consumption · Basic services

Using Basic Services Responsibly

Guiding question

“How can my family, my school and I use water and electricity without waste?”

Learning target: I can tell needs from waste, calculate simple consumption and savings, carry out a simulated audit at school, and make a saving commitment I can check.

Unit indicator: Shows habits of responsible consumption in the use of basic services in the family, the school and the community.  ·  Textbook: Creciendo en Valores 8, pp. 66–70 and 113–114

Basic services are shared resources

Basic services are things we need every day: drinking water, electricity, sanitation and waste collection. They come from limited resources and they are paid for by families and by the community (through fees and taxes). Responsible consumption means using what we need and avoiding waste.

The textbook teaches that we love and care for our home, school and community, and that a responsible citizen evaluates the impact of their decisions and uses natural resources rationally.

Where I take care: home, school, community

HomeTidy my room; pick up clothes and toys; take out the trash; sweep and mop; water the plants.
SchoolHelp clean the board; clean desks and furniture; sweep the classroom daily; empty the classroom trash bins; do not scribble on walls; do not slam doors.
CommunitySweep gutters and throw out trash along the street; cut weeds in parks; keep public spaces clean.
