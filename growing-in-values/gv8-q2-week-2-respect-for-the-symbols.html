Respecting and Using the Symbols Properly

Guiding question

“How can I show respect for the flag and the anthem at school and on screen?”

Learning target: I can explain the rules for hoisting, lowering, folding and placing the flag and for using the anthem, judge school acts and digital posts, and write a decalogue of respectful use.

Unit indicator: Shows respect for the patriotic and national symbols in the family, the school and the community.  ·  Textbook: Creciendo en Valores 8, pp. 90–96

Respect is something we do

The law on the characteristics and use of the patriotic symbols tells us how to treat the flag and the anthem. But the law is only the minimum. Real respect is an attitude: we stand properly, sing with feeling, take care of the symbols and do not use them as a joke or as advertising.

This week you will study the rules, practice them, and write a decalogue (ten rules) for the proper use of the symbols at school and online.

The right posture

When the anthem is sungStand straight and still, sing with feeling, and understand each word. A hand over the heart is a respectful way to salute.
When the flag is hoisted or loweredStop and face the flag. Remove any head covering with the right hand and keep your arms parallel to your body, or place the right hand, palm down, at the height of the heart.
