Cyclists and Traffic Signs

Guiding question

“What do traffic signs tell us, and how can a cyclist stay safe and visible?”

Learning target: I can classify vertical traffic signs by shape and color, explain the rules for riding a bicycle, and design a sign that teaches a safe behavior.

Unit indicator: Shows respect for the road education rules as a pedestrian, cyclist and passenger in the places where they travel.  ·  Textbook: Creciendo en Valores 8, pp. 124–127

Signs speak a language everyone can read

Traffic signs communicate messages that tell pedestrians and drivers the correct and safe way to travel on public roads. Their shape and color tell us what kind of message it is, even before we read the symbol.

Cyclists must also follow the rules. “Take care of your life, protect yourself, use the helmet.” (textbook, p. 125)

The three kinds of vertical signs

Preventive (warning)Diamond shape, black symbol on a yellow background. They warn drivers that there is danger on the road.
RegulatoryCircular, black symbol, white background and a red border. They state limits and prohibitions. Not obeying them is an infraction.
InformativeRectangular, blue or green background with a white frame. They identify roads and guide the driver and give useful data for the trip.
