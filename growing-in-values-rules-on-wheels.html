Respecting Traffic as a Pedestrian, Cyclist and Passenger

Guiding question: What rule protects me in each of my roles in traffic: as a pedestrian, as a cyclist, and as a passenger?

Every time we move around our community we play a role in traffic. Each role has specific safety rules. Respecting them protects you and the people around you: this is responsibility and respect for life. (Tap each role to see its rules.)

Key takeaways
  • Each role has its own rules: pedestrian, cyclist, passenger.
  • Rules are not only about avoiding a fine. They make traffic predictable, so everyone can anticipate what others will do.
  • Respecting a rule protects the person and others.

Traffic rules vary from country to country. Ask your teacher about the rules where you live.
