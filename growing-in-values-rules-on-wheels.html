8th Grade · Rules on wheels⏱ about 3 min

Warm-up: Which Role, Which Rule?

Guiding question: What rule protects me in each of my roles in traffic: as a pedestrian, as a cyclist, and as a passenger?
By the end of this activity you can: identify the safety rules for pedestrians, cyclists and passengers · show respect for those rules in a case or role-play · write a personal commitment to follow them.

Quick check (not scored). For each scene, name the role and say whether the rule was respected.

Review⏱ about 2 min

Rules by Role

Every time we move around our community we play a role in traffic. Each role has specific safety rules. (Tap each role to see its rules.)

Key takeaways
  • Each role has its own rules: pedestrian, cyclist, passenger.
  • Respecting a rule protects you and others.
  • Rules vary by country, so ask your teacher about the rules where you live.
Review⏱ about 2 min

Why Do Rules Protect Us?

Rules are not only about avoiding a fine. They show respect for life and responsibility toward everyone who shares the road. Tap each card, think of your answer first, then reveal it.

Guided Example: Sofia the Cyclist

Case: Sofia rides her bike to school. She rides on the side of the road facing the traffic, wears no helmet, and turns at the corner without signaling.

Solved case 2 of 3 · Pedestrian⏱ about 2 min

Guided Example: Leo the Pedestrian

Case: Leo walks home from school wearing headphones and looking at his phone. At the corner the pedestrian signal is red. He sees no cars, so he steps off the curb and crosses diagonally, away from the crosswalk, without looking both ways.

This time, try to predict each step before you reveal it.

Solved case 3 of 3 · Passenger⏱ about 2 min

Guided Example: Ana the Passenger

Case: Ana is in the back seat of her uncle's car. She does not buckle up because the trip is short, leans forward to show the driver a video, and when they arrive she opens the street-side door without checking for traffic.

Use the same steps. Notice how many rules were broken in one short trip.
