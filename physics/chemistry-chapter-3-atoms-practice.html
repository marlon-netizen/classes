Reminders Z (atomic number) = number of protons.
A (mass number) = protons + neutrons, so neutrons = A − Z.
Neutral atom: electrons = protons.
Ion: charge = protons − electrons.
Isotopes: same Z, different A.
Writing answers Hyphen notation: Carbon-14
Nuclear symbol: A on top, Z at the bottom, then the symbol (capital first letter).
Charges: +2, −1 or 0 (also 2+, 1-).
Big/small numbers: 6.02x10^23 or 3.5e-7.
Periodic table helper: atomic number, symbol, name and molar mass (g/mol)
