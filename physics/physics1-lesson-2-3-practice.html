25 free-fall exercises

These exercises practise everything from Lesson 2.3 (Falling Objects): dropped objects, objects thrown up or down, other worlds, and the equations for constant acceleration. Your numbers are your own: every student gets a different set of 25 exercises, all of the same level of difficulty, so work on your own.

  • Use up = positive and ag = −9.81 m/s² (so a falling object has a negative velocity and a negative displacement). When a question asks for a height, distance or time, give a positive number.
  • Type your answer and press Check (or Enter). Answers within about 3 % are accepted, and you can write numbers like 2.2, −3.5e-3 or 1.38 x 10^4. You can try again as many times as you like.
  • Stuck? After two tries you can open the worked solution.
  • All your answers are saved (every try, right or wrong), so your teacher can read them and you can continue on any device.
