25 vector exercises

These exercises practise everything from Lessons 3.1 and 3.2: scalars and vectors, adding and subtracting vectors, perpendicular vectors (Pythagorean theorem and tangent), components (sine and cosine) and adding vectors that are not perpendicular. Your numbers are your own: every student gets a different set of 25 exercises, all of the same level of difficulty, so work on your own.

  • Use east and north as positive unless an exercise says otherwise, and set your calculator to degrees. When a question asks for a magnitude, distance or angle, give a positive number; when it asks for a component or a displacement along a line, give a signed value.
  • Type your answer and press Check (or Enter). Answers within about 3 % are accepted, and you can write numbers like 2.2, −3.5e-3 or 35.4°. You can try again as many times as you like.
  • Stuck? After two tries you can open the worked solution.
  • All your answers are saved (every try, right or wrong), so your teacher can read them and you can continue on any device.
